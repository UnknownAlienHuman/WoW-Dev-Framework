# E4-B lineage candidate router

The canonical supporting search seam is [`e4/LINEAGE_CANDIDATE_HANDOFF.md`](e4/LINEAGE_CANDIDATE_HANDOFF.md).

`wow-search` produces bounded exact-generation `search_lineage_candidate` partitions only. Every cross-generation pair remains `Candidate` regardless of exact-name band, search rank, signal count, signature/fingerprint similarity, graph neighborhood, or uniqueness. Accepted lineage, replacement, removal, migration and impact remain owned by the E4-B graph contract and future E4-C orchestration.
